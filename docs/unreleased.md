## Themes

- **Three new Newspaper-inspired themes modelled on real mastheads: `gotham`,
  `manhattan`, `city`.**
  - `gotham` (The New York Times) — black on white, slab-serif Bitter
    headlines over a Georgia-style Noto Serif body, classic newsprint-blue
    accent.
  - `manhattan` (The Wall Street Journal) — cream newsprint, Zilla Slab
    headlines over an EB Garamond body, deep masthead-red accent.
  - `city` (The Financial Times) — the signature salmon page background
    site-wide, a Frank Ruhl Libre display serif for headlines over a clean
    IBM Plex Sans body, teal accent.
  Registered in `sample-sites/themes.json` alongside the existing entries.
