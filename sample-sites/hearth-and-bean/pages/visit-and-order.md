# Visit & Order

## Visit Us

**Address:** 14 Marlow Street, Elmsworth

**Hours:**

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday – Friday | 7:00am – 6:00pm |
| Saturday | 8:00am – 6:00pm |
| Sunday | 8:00am – 3:00pm |

The roaster runs Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday mornings from about 8am — come early if you want to see (and smell) it working. Seating is first-come; eighteen seats, a bar along the espresso machine, and one long communal table at the back. We're a genuinely good place to work from, and there's no time limit on a table — just be considerate if it's a busy Saturday morning.

**Getting here:** two blocks from the Elmsworth Green transit stop, street parking on Marlow itself and free after 6pm, small bike rack out front.

**Accessibility:** step-free entrance, one accessible table by the window, restroom is accessible.

## Order Ahead

Skip the line by ordering ahead through our online ordering page — pick a time and pay in advance, then walk straight to the pickup end of the bar. Available during all opening hours.

## Beans, Delivered

We ship 250g bags nationally, roasted to order and shipped within 24 hours of roasting so it arrives near the start of its flavour window, not the end.

**Subscriptions:**

| Plan | What You Get | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Roaster's Choice | Whatever's best on the roast schedule that week — our pick | Weekly or fortnightly |
| Single Origin Only | Always a single-origin lot, never the espresso blend | Weekly or fortnightly |
| Espresso Blend | Our house espresso blend, every time | Weekly or fortnightly |

Pause, skip, or cancel any time from your account — no minimum commitment. Whole bean or ground to your brew method, your choice at signup.

## Wholesale

We supply beans to a small number of local restaurants, offices, and other cafés, with training on our recommended brew parameters included for espresso accounts. Volume pricing starts at 5kg/week. Get in touch below to talk through fit and lead times.

## Catering & Events

We do pour-over and cold brew service for offices, weddings, and private events — bring-your-own setup or full service with a barista, your call. We also host public cupping sessions on the first Saturday of every month at 10am, free, no booking required, first ten people through the door.
