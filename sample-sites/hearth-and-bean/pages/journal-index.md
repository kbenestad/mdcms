# The Journal

Notes from the roastery: new single-origin lots as they arrive, brewing guides for the methods we use at the bar, and the occasional deeper dive when something's worth explaining properly.

## Single-Origin Releases

We post a short write-up every time a new lot goes on the filter bar — where it's from, how it was processed, and what to expect in the cup. If you want to know what's pouring right now, this is also the fastest way to check before you come in.

## Brewing Guides

Step-by-step guides for the methods we use ourselves: V60, AeroPress, Chemex, and cold brew, plus the reasoning behind each recipe so you can adapt it rather than just follow it.

## Coffee Science

Longer explainers that didn't fit on [the nerdy stuff](pages/the-nerdy-stuff.md) page — water chemistry deep dives, roast curve breakdowns, and the odd myth we felt like debunking.

## Notes from the Roastery

Sourcing trips, roast day stories, and the occasional mistake we learned something from.
