# Welcome to Hearth & Bean

We roast in small batches, brew with more care than is strictly necessary, and are always happy to talk about water chemistry with anyone who asks — and several people who didn't.

Hearth & Bean is a coffee roastery and café on Marlow Street. We roast twice a week, pull espresso all day, and keep a rotating lineup of single-origin filter coffee for anyone who wants to taste a farm rather than a blend. If you want a straightforward oat flat white, we will make you a very good one. If you want to know why the Kenyan lot tastes like blackcurrant, we will happily keep you at the counter for twenty minutes explaining processing methods.

## A Note on What We're Doing Here

We are not trying to reinvent coffee. Filter and espresso have been done well for a long time; our job is mostly to buy good green coffee, roast it competently, and not get in its way. Where we spend our energy is on the things that are easy to get wrong quietly — grind consistency, water quality, dose accuracy, freshness — because those are the variables that separate a fine cup from a forgettable one, and most of them are invisible unless someone tells you to look for them.

Come in, sit down, and ask us anything. We like the questions as much as the coffee.
