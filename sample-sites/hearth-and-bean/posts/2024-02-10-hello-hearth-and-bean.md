We roast, we brew, and apparently now we also write. This journal is where we'll post a short note every time a new single-origin lot lands on the filter bar, plus brewing guides for the methods we use at the bar and the occasional longer piece when something's worth explaining properly.

If you've been in the shop and asked one of us a question about extraction, water, or roast curves and gotten a longer answer than you bargained for — this is basically that, in writing, so you can go back and reread it later.

First real post coming this week: our new washed Kenyan just landed, and it's one of the best lots we've had from that region.
