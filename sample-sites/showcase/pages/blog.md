# Blog

## Reverse-chronological, paginated

Generated by `posts-created-reversechronological`. It reads every file in
`posts/`, sorts by the `created` frontmatter field (newest first), and
paginates with a "load more"-style pager (`paginate: yes`).

```mdcms
posts-created-reversechronological
limit: 5
paginate: yes
```

## Chronological, grouped by year and month

The other reliable variant: `posts-created-chronological-byyearmonth`.
Oldest first, grouped under a heading per year-month — good for a
changelog-style archive. `paginate: none` caps the *group* count, not the
total post count.

```mdcms
posts-created-chronological-byyearmonth
paginate: none
```

## The full grammar

```text
posts-created-<order>[-<modifier>]
  order:    chronological | reversechronological
  modifier: byyear | byyearmonth | lastyear | lastmonth   (optional)
```

Only the two variants demonstrated above are reliable — avoid the rest until
they're fixed. `limit` caps how many posts show at once (or the batch size
under pagination); `paginate` is `yes` (load-more), `none` (hard cap, no
control), or `no` (show everything, the default).
